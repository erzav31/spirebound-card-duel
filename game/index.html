A CARD DUEL OF THE FOUR WINDS

SPIREBOUND

Four winds. One spire. Your move.

Climb the spire and dethrone Zephyra, its Warden. Summon creatures of Ember, Tide, Gale and Stone, chain same-wind creatures into Resonance, and bring her life to zero before she brings yours.

▲ Ember — burns fast, hits hard
● Tide — endures, heals, outlasts
◆ Gale — swift, draws, races ahead
■ Stone — stands, quakes, crushes
Resonance — a creature gains +1 attack and +1 health for each other friendly creature of the same wind.
Mana — you gain one crystal a turn, up to 10. Unspent mana is lost.
Combat — creatures strike back when attacked. New summons rest a turn.
Both duelists start at 30 life · Decks of 24 · Zephyra draws an extra card for going second · First to zero loses
