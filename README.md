**SPIREBOUND — Duel of the Four Winds.** An original turn-based card dueling game: climb the spire and dethrone Zephyra, its Warden. Summon creatures of Ember, Tide, Gale and Stone, chain same-wind creatures into Resonance (+1 attack and +1 health per matching ally), and bring her 30 life to zero before she brings yours. Both duelists draw from fair 24-card decks; the AI plays, trades and goes for lethal with a readable plan you can counter.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network — the game is plain static files under `game/`.

Keyboard: `1–8` play a hand creature, `Enter` ends the turn, `Esc` cancels a selection.
